## Overview

TechnoSense NextGen Solutions collaborates with technology leaders as part of its approach to delivering secure, scalable, and enterprise-grade IT solutions.

The official TechnoSense homepage contains a **"Trusted alliances"** section stating that the company collaborates with technology leaders to deliver secure, scalable, enterprise-grade outcomes.

The technology organizations displayed in this section are:

- AWS
- Microsoft
- Redington
- Fortinet

These are the technology alliances explicitly presented by TechnoSense on its official website.



# Trusted Alliances

AWS is therefore explicitly represented by TechnoSense as a partner/alliance relationship.

# Technology Partner vs Technology Expertise

The RAG system must distinguish between **technology partnerships** and **technology expertise**.

For example:

```text
Technology Partner
       ↓
Explicitly identified by TechnoSense
       ↓
AWS / Microsoft / Redington / Fortinet
```

Whereas:

```text
Technology Expertise
       ↓
Technology used or supported by TechnoSense
       ↓
Oracle / APEX / ADF / Database / etc.
```

A technology appearing in TechnoSense's service portfolio does not automatically mean that the company has a formal partnership with that technology vendor.

This distinction is important for accurate company-specific RAG responses.

# Important Answer Boundaries

The following relationships are explicitly supported by the TechnoSense website:

- **AWS — AWS Partner**
- **Microsoft — Trusted Alliance**
- **Redington — Trusted Alliance**
- **Fortinet — Trusted Alliance**

The published material does **not** establish:

- Specific partner tiers
- Partnership certification levels
- Number of certifications
- Specific vendor competencies
- Partnership duration
- Contractual arrangements
- Exclusivity
- Revenue-sharing arrangements
- Vendor-specific pricing
- Specific vendor products used in every project
- Guaranteed access to vendor resources
- Current partner status beyond what is published on the website

The RAG system must not invent these details.

Most importantly:

> **A technology appearing in TechnoSense's service portfolio should not automatically be classified as a technology partnership.**

For example, TechnoSense demonstrates significant Oracle technology expertise through services such as Oracle ADF, Oracle APEX, and Oracle Database. However, the available Trusted Alliances section does not list Oracle among the four organizations displayed there.

Therefore, Oracle should be represented as a **technology/service area**, not automatically as a **confirmed technology partner**, unless TechnoSense publishes an explicit Oracle partnership elsewhere.

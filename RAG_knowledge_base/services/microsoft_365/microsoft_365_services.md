## Overview

TechnoSense provides Microsoft Office 365 services to help organizations improve productivity, collaboration, security, and management of their digital workplace.

The company's Microsoft O365 offering covers the broader lifecycle of an Office 365 environment, including licensing, initial setup, migration, security configuration, and mobile device management.

TechnoSense states that its Microsoft 365 expertise includes deployment, configuration, security, and management. The company follows Microsoft best practices and security guidelines to help organizations optimize their O365 environments while maintaining security and compliance.

## Service Portfolio

TechnoSense identifies three primary services within its Microsoft O365 portfolio:

1. **O365 Licensing & Setup**
2. **Security Solutions**
3. **Mobile Devices Management**

These services collectively address Microsoft 365 adoption, deployment, security, and device management.
